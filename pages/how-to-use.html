01

Uploading Your Document

Getting started is simple. Norcanto AI accepts PDF, DOCX, and TXT files up to 20MB. You can drag and drop your file directly onto the upload area or click to browse your device.

Supported formats: PDF, DOCX, and TXT files
Maximum file size is 20MB per document
Text-based PDFs produce the best analysis results
Your documents are processed securely and never shared

Understanding Your Summary

Once analysis is complete, Norcanto AI delivers a structured intelligence report. The Executive Summary gives you a concise overview, while the Plain Language Explanation translates complex jargon into clear, simple terms.

Executive Summary covers the most important points

Key Insights highlight the most critical findings

03

Reviewing Risks and Obligations

Norcanto AI automatically identifies potential risks, assigns severity levels, and suggests mitigation actions. Obligations are separately listed so you know exactly what you or the other party must do.

Risks rated High, Medium, or Low by severity
Each risk includes a suggested mitigation action
Obligations list what each party must do
Risk detection requires Premium or Trial plan

04

Using AI Document Chat

The AI chat panel lets you ask any question about your document and receive precise answers that reference specific sections. Use the suggested questions to get started, or ask anything you need to know.

Suggested questions appear automatically based on document type
Answers reference the source section when available
Press Enter to send, Shift+Enter for a new line
Chat message limits depend on your plan
Ask the Document

05

Exporting Your Analysis

Premium and Pro plan users can export the full analysis report as a text file. The export includes the executive summary, all insights, risks, obligations, and action items in a clean, readable format.

Export available on Premium and Pro plans
Click the Export button in the analysis view top bar
The report downloads as a structured text file
Useful for sharing with colleagues or legal teams
