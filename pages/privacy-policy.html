Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025. Effective date: January 1, 2025.

This Privacy Policy explains how Norcanto ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use Norcanto AI at norcanto.com. By using Norcanto AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and information generated through your use of the service.

  • Account information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and a hashed version of your password.
  • Document content: Documents you upload are temporarily processed to generate AI analysis. We do not permanently store document content.
  • Usage data: We track the number of documents analyzed, AI requests made, and features used to maintain reliability and improve the service.
  • Technical data: IP addresses, browser type, operating system, and pages visited to ensure security and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve Norcanto AI
  • To protect service reliability and prevent abuse
  • To send important account and service notifications
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Document Processing and AI

When you upload a document, its content is sent to Google Gemini AI via Google's API for analysis. This processing is governed by Google's data processing terms in addition to this policy. We do not use your document content to train AI models. Processed documents are not permanently stored on our servers.

Analysis results are stored in your account history so you can access them later. You can delete your analysis history at any time from your dashboard.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use Supabase (database), Google Gemini (AI processing), and Mailgun (email). Each provider is bound by data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.
  • Business transfers: If Norcanto is acquired or merged, your data may transfer to the acquiring entity subject to the same privacy protections.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Analysis history is retained until you delete it. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain it.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), hashed passwords, API authentication tokens, and server-side validation. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and preferences. We use analytics cookies to understand how users interact with Norcanto AI. See our Cookie Policy for details.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You may also request a copy of your data or ask us to restrict processing in certain circumstances. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@norcanto.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Norcanto AI is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we learn we have collected such information, we will promptly delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or by posting a notice on Norcanto AI. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact us at hello@norcanto.com or visit our Contact page.
