The Complete Guide to AI Document Chat

Asking the right questions of an AI document chat tool means the difference between surface-level summaries and genuinely useful deep analysis. Here are the techniques that work.

N
Norcanto Team
·December 9, 2024·8 min read
Key Takeaways
  • Specific questions get specific answers — vague questions produce vague summaries
  • Reference exact sections or clause numbers for the most precise responses
  • Ask the AI to compare, contrast, and find inconsistencies across the document
  • Follow-up questions that build on previous answers produce deeper analysis
  • Ask for the AI's confidence level when a claim seems uncertain

When most people first use an AI document chat tool, they ask broad questions: "Summarize this contract" or "What are the risks?" These are fine starting points. But the real power of document chat emerges when you ask specific, targeted questions that the AI can answer with precision by referencing exact parts of the document.

Here is a systematic approach to getting the most out of AI document chat.

The Principle of Specificity

AI document chat is essentially a very fast, very thorough research assistant that has read your entire document. Like a human research assistant, it produces better results when given specific tasks than vague ones.

Compare these two questions about a service agreement:

  • Vague: "What does this contract say about payments?"
  • Specific: "What is the exact payment schedule in Section 4, and what happens if a payment is missed?"

The second question will produce a far more useful answer because it points the AI to a specific section and asks for specific information, including a follow-on implication that requires reasoning across the document.

Question Types That Produce the Best Results

Definition questions: "How does this contract define 'Confidential Information'?" These work extremely well because definitions are usually explicit in documents.

Consequence questions: "What happens if either party breaches Section 8?" These require reasoning about cause and effect within the document.

Comparison questions: "Does the liability cap in Section 7 apply to the indemnification obligation in Section 9, or does Section 9 create an exception?" These cross-section questions are where AI chat really earns its value.

Completeness questions: "Is there a force majeure clause in this contract?" or "Does this NDA cover oral disclosures as well as written ones?" These verification questions are useful for checking whether standard provisions are present.

Implication questions: "If we terminate this contract under Section 12.2, what obligations survive termination?" These require the AI to synthesize information from multiple sections.

The Follow-Up Technique

The best document chat sessions are conversations, not single questions. Start with a broad question to orient yourself, then drill down with follow-up questions based on the answer.

Example conversation with a contract:

  • Q: "What are the termination provisions in this contract?"
  • A: [AI describes termination for cause, termination for convenience, and the notice requirements]
  • Q: "If we terminate for convenience, what payments are we obligated to make to the other party?"
  • A: [AI gives specific answer about termination fees or wind-down obligations]
  • Q: "Is that termination fee capped, or is it based on the full remaining contract value?"
  • A: [AI confirms whether there is a cap and where it is defined]

This layered approach produces far more useful analysis than a single broad question.

Pro tip: When you get an answer that references a specific section, ask the AI to quote or closely paraphrase the relevant language from that section. This lets you verify the AI's interpretation against the actual text.

Questions to Ask About Any Contract

These questions are worth asking about almost any commercial contract:

  • "What are the exact payment terms and what are the consequences of late payment?"
  • "What is the liability cap and does it have any exceptions?"
  • "What provisions survive termination of this agreement?"
  • "What representations does each party make about themselves?"
  • "Does this contract include any exclusivity or non-compete restrictions?"
  • "Who owns the intellectual property created under this agreement?"
  • "What events allow either party to terminate without cause?"
  • "Is there an automatic renewal provision and what is the opt-out window?"

When AI Chat Has Limitations

AI document chat is highly reliable for questions that can be answered by reading and reasoning about the document itself. It is less reliable for questions that require external context — industry norms, jurisdictional legal interpretations, or information not present in the document. When the AI is uncertain, good tools will say so. Pay attention to hedging language in answers and verify important claims directly in the document when it matters.
