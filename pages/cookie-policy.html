Last updated: January 1, 2025.

This Cookie Policy explains how Norcanto ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit Norcanto AI. By continuing to use Norcanto AI, you consent to the use of cookies as described here.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit, such as your login status and preferences. We also use localStorage, a browser-based storage mechanism, to store session data on your device.

2. Cookies We Use

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may set their own cookies or use tracking technologies:

  • Google Gemini API: Used server-side for AI processing. No client-side cookies are set by this service.
  • Supabase: Our database provider. Used server-side only.
  • Netlify: Our hosting provider may collect anonymized access logs for performance and security.

4. Managing Cookies

You can clear localStorage and cookies at any time through your browser settings. Clearing essential cookies will sign you out of Norcanto AI and remove locally stored documents. You can also decline non-essential cookies using the cookie consent banner shown on your first visit.

Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or clear them. For instructions specific to your browser, visit its help documentation.
