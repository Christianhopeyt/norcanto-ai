About

Built to Make Documents
Work For You

Norcanto AI is a product of Norcanto, a technology company focused on building practical AI tools that save professionals time and reduce the risk of missing critical information in documents.

Mission

We believe no one should have to read a 40-page contract twice.

Every day, professionals across every industry spend hours reading through dense documents, contracts, research papers, and reports. Most of that time is spent searching for the handful of facts that actually matter.

Norcanto AI was built to change that. We use advanced AI to instantly surface what matters, flag what is risky, and tell you exactly what you need to do next.

Technology

Powered by Google Gemini AI

Norcanto AI is built on Google Gemini, one of the most capable large language models available. Gemini enables us to understand context across long documents, identify nuanced legal and financial risks, and generate structured, accurate intelligence reports.

Our AI service layer is designed to be model-agnostic, meaning we can update or switch underlying models as the technology evolves without any change to the user experience.

Values

What We Stand For

Clarity First
Every output from Norcanto AI is designed to be immediately understandable. We strip away ambiguity and give you clear, structured information you can act on.
Privacy by Design
We do not store your documents permanently. We do not train AI models on your data. Your documents are your own, and we treat them that way.
Accessible to Everyone
Document intelligence should not require a legal team or a data science department. Norcanto AI puts the same power in the hands of individuals and small teams.
