FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Norcanto AI currently supports PDF, DOCX (Microsoft Word), and TXT files. Maximum file size is 20MB. We are actively working to add support for additional formats including Excel spreadsheets and PowerPoint presentations.
Norcanto AI uses Google Gemini, one of the most capable AI models available. Analysis accuracy depends on document clarity and complexity. For legal or financial decisions, we recommend using Norcanto AI insights as a starting point and consulting qualified professionals for final decisions.
Your documents are processed securely and not stored permanently on our servers. Document content is sent to Google Gemini API for analysis and is subject to Google's data handling policies. We do not use your documents to train AI models. For sensitive documents, you control which documents you upload.
Most documents are analyzed in 15-60 seconds depending on length and complexity. A standard 10-page contract typically takes under 30 seconds. Longer academic papers or comprehensive reports may take up to 90 seconds.
